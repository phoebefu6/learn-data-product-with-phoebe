Gen AI - governing and amplifying the whole stack

This is the hardest rung, and the most misunderstood. The gen-AI tier is not "add a chatbot" - it is two things at once: putting real governance and quality controls under everything below, and amplifying the work above with automation and agents. The twist you will feel in this session: governance and data quality are foundational and cross-cutting, yet they live here because the gen-AI era is what finally gives them teeth. LLMs and agents act on your data at scale and amplify any rot, so the "nice to have" controls become the difference between a helpful agent and a confident liar. Six products, Kasa's support agent as the flagship.

Part 1 · govern the foundation

The controls that earn the right to automate 17 min live

Before Kasa lets any agent act, two products have to be solid: Data Governance (who owns what, who may touch it, how it is protected) and a Data Quality Scorecard (a live health score that gates what the AI may use). Both are foundational and cross-cutting - they run through the whole stack - but they get real teeth here, because gen AI acts on data at machine speed and amplifies whatever it finds.

LiveData Governance5 min▶
What it isThe policies, roles, and controls for how data is owned, accessed, protected, and used - the rulebook that says who may touch what, and how it is kept safe.
Kasa examplePDPA-compliant access and full lineage on Kasa Pay wallet data - so the support agent can only see what policy allows, and every access is traceable.
Business valueTrust, compliance, and safe AI. It is the licence to automate at all in a regulated fintech.
Reach for it whenData is regulated, shared across teams, or feeding AI. In practice: the moment agents or LLMs start acting on it.
Tier chip🔴 Gen AI · foundational, teeth here
Why governance is parked in the gen-AI tier - not papered over Governance is genuinely foundational and cross-cutting; it belongs under every rung. It sits here because the gen-AI era is what makes it unignorable. A human reading an ungoverned dashboard is a slow risk. An agent acting on ungoverned data - issuing refunds, emailing customers, drafting board notes - amplifies any rot instantly and at scale. Gen AI does not create the need for governance; it removes the option of skipping it.
LiveData Quality Scorecard4 min▶
What it isA live health score of your data across completeness, accuracy, and timeliness - quality made visible, tracked, and reportable rather than assumed.
Kasa exampleA quality score that gates what the AI agent is allowed to use - if the order table is stale or incomplete, the agent falls back to a human instead of answering on rotten data.
Business valueQuality becomes visible and fundable - leaders can see the number, so they can pay to fix it. It also becomes the safety gate for automation.
Reach for it whenQuality problems keep surfacing downstream, or you are about to let software act on data automatically.
Tier chip🔴 Gen AI · foundational, teeth here
The scorecard is the agent's seatbelt Like governance, data quality is cross-cutting - it matters at every tier. But in the gen-AI era it becomes a live gate: the agent reads the quality score before it acts. Green, it answers; red, it escalates. That single check is what stops "amplify the work" from becoming "amplify the errors".
Part 2 · lift the people

Before you deploy AI, get the org ready for it 14 min live

Governance earns the right to automate; people decide whether it lands. Two products lift the human side: AI Literacy Training (so staff use and question AI safely) and an AI Use Case Discovery Workshop (so the org spends on the right ideas, not the loudest). Both are cheap relative to the automation they de-risk.

LiveAI Literacy Training3 min▶
What it isLifting the whole org's ability to use and question AI safely - not just prompt tricks, but knowing when the model is likely wrong and when not to trust it.
Kasa exampleStaff learn prompt basics and, more importantly, when NOT to trust the model - so a store manager double-checks a generated figure instead of pasting it into a board pack.
Business valueAdoption without incidents. The org gets the upside of AI tools without the confident-wrong-answer disasters.
Reach for it whenEveryone suddenly has AI tools - copilots in every app - and nobody has taught them the failure modes.
Tier chip🔴 Gen AI · human on-ramp
Literacy is the sibling of Session 4's data literacy Data literacy taught the org to read a number; AI literacy teaches it to question a generated answer. Same pattern - lift the people before the tools outrun them. Go deeper in Phoebe's learn-ai-literacy course.
LiveAI Use Case Discovery Workshop4 min▶
What it isA structured session that finds and prioritizes AI opportunities by value and feasibility - turning "we should use AI" into a ranked, fundable shortlist.
Kasa exampleScore 20 candidate ideas on a value-vs-feasibility grid, then pick the two that win: the support bot and a fraud copilot for the Kasa Pay risk team.
Business valueFocus and buy-in before spend. It kills pet projects early and gets leadership aligned on where the money goes.
Reach for it whenLeadership says "we should use AI" with no plan, or every team is lobbying for its own pet model.
Tier chip🔴 Gen AI · prioritization
Part 3 · amplify the work

Where gen AI actually does the work 11 min live

Now the payoff. With governance under it and people ready, Kasa amplifies real work: Workflow Automation handles the repetitive, rule-ish steps, and Agentic AI - the flagship - plans and acts across tools toward a goal, with guardrails. This is the top of the ladder, and it only holds because every rung beneath it is solid.

LiveWorkflow Automation3 min▶
What it isAI and rules automating a repeatable multi-step process end to end - the steps a person used to click through by hand.
Kasa exampleAuto-triage of incoming support tickets plus AI-drafted replies - the ticket is classified, routed, and a suggested response is waiting for the agent to approve.
Business valueHours saved and faster response times, with humans reviewing rather than typing from scratch.
Reach for it whenA process is repetitive and rule-ish - the same steps, most of the time, with clear inputs and outputs.
Tier chip🔴 Gen AI · amplify
Real world

Kasa's support queue used to pile up overnight - every ticket read, sorted, and answered by hand each morning. Workflow automation now triages each ticket the moment it lands (billing, delivery, returns, fraud), routes it to the right queue, and drafts a reply from the knowledge base. Agents open their morning to a sorted queue with drafts to approve, not a wall of unread tickets. First-response time dropped from hours to minutes, and no ticket falls through the cracks. Crucially, it is still rule-ish - a fixed process, faster - which is exactly the line between automation and the agentic AI below.

LiveAgentic AI ★ flagship · 4 min▶
What it isAI that plans and acts across tools and steps toward a goal, with guardrails - it decides what to do next, not just fills in a fixed script.
Kasa exampleThe support agent behind the app: a customer asks "where is my order?" and it checks order status across systems; "refund me" and it issues the refund if it is within policy, and escalates the edge cases to a human.
Business value24/7 resolution with a human in the loop for anything risky - lower cost to serve, faster answers, and staff freed for the hard cases.
Reach for it whenAutomation needs judgment and tool use, not a fixed script - the path branches, and the right next step depends on what the last step found.
Tier chip🔴 Gen AI · flagship, top of the ladder
Real world

This is why the whole ladder had to be climbed first. Kasa's support agent can safely say "I have refunded your order" only because the warehouse tells it the true order status, the data-quality scorecard is green, and governance defines exactly what a refund within policy means and how much it may issue without a human. The guardrail check in the loop is not decoration - it is the difference between a helpful agent and one that confidently refunds a fraudster. Pull out any lower rung and the agent acts fluently on fiction. That dependency is the entire argument of this course, and it lands hardest right here at the top.

Before Session 6

This week ◐ 25 min total

  • Audit your governance teeth. Name one dataset an AI tool at your org can already act on. Is access governed and lineage traceable, or would you find out only after something went wrong? That gap is your teeth-in-the-gen-AI-era test.
  • Draw your quality gate. Pick one place you would let software act automatically. What single data-quality check should block it when the data is stale or incomplete? Write the green-answer / red-escalate rule.
  • Run a mini use-case grid. List three "we should use AI" ideas floating around your org and place them on value-vs-feasibility. Which one actually wins, and which is just loud?
  • Optional: map one repetitive process (triage, reporting, replies) and mark where it is rule-ish (workflow automation) versus where it needs judgment and branching (agentic AI). The line between them is the lesson.

Three questions before you go 🎯 ◐ 90 seconds

1 · Data Governance and Data Quality are foundational and cross-cutting. Why are they taught in the gen-AI tier?

Governance and quality touch every rung of the ladder. They live in the gen-AI tier because gen AI is what makes ignoring them dangerous: when software acts on your behalf, weak controls get amplified instantly and confidently.

2 · What separates Workflow Automation from Agentic AI?

Workflow automation speeds up a repeatable, mostly-fixed process (triage + draft replies). Agentic AI is for when the path branches and the right next action depends on what the last step found - it needs judgment and tool use, not a script.

3 · Kasa's support agent can safely say "I have refunded your order" only because...

The agent sits at the top of the ladder and depends on every rung below. The guardrail check - within policy and quality green - is the difference between a helpful agent and one that confidently refunds a fraudster.
