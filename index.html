Learn Data Product with Phoebe

Twenty-nine data products, one maturity ladder, told through a company growing up.

A guided tour of what a modern data and AI function actually builds - from cloud infrastructure to agentic AI - shown through Kasa, a made-up Southeast Asian retailer with an embedded payments wallet, as it climbs from emailed spreadsheets to one governed platform. This is a capability catalog, not a build-it-yourself bootcamp: every product gets the same card - what it is, a Kasa example, the business value, and when to reach for it - so a leader can commission it and a builder can place it. Four tiers, six sessions, one running case. Read it top to bottom, or jump to the rung your org is stuck on.

One running case: Kasa. Every session works on Kasa - a fictional Southeast Asian omnichannel retailer with ~4M customers, 180 stores across SG, MY, ID and PH, a shopping app, and Kasa Pay, an embedded loyalty wallet with a small buy-now-pay-later line (~US$1.2B revenue). We meet it in chaos - every team with its own "revenue" number, POS data trapped per store, the wallet bleeding to fraud, leadership demanding "AI" before the warehouse exists - and grow it up one tier at a time into a single governed platform.
Honest about scope. This is a capability catalog for leaders and the curious, not a hands-on build course. Each of the 29 products is taught at the "what it is / example / value / when to reach for it" level - enough to commission it and place it on the ladder, not to build it from scratch. The deep how-to lives in Phoebe's other courses on the hub (DataOps, SQL, Python data analysis, statistics, experimentation, RAG, LangChain, evals, and the governance bucket). Tool names appear as examples only; the course is tool-agnostic by design. Content verified July 2026.
